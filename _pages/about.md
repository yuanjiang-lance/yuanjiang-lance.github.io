**Hi, I'm glad you're here!** 

I'm a 4th-year Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Engineering at the [Department of Industrial & Enterprise Systems Engineering](https://ise.illinois.edu/), [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/), advised by [Prof. Pingfeng Wang](https://ise.illinois.edu/directory/profile/pingfeng). Prior to UIUC, I received both my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Vehicle Engineering from the [Institute of Rail Transit](https://railway.tongji.edu.cn/) at [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/), where I worked with [Prof. Gang Niu](https://tjjt.tongji.edu.cn/info/2943/10978.htm).

My research lies at the intersection of **physics-informed AI and digital twin**, with a focus on design, manufacturing, and lifecycle prognostics and health management of complex engineering systems. It addresses a central question: **How can we understand, design, manufacture, and manage physical systems when neither physics nor real-world data alone are sufficient?** I develop **physics-informed, model-aware, and data-efficient computational methods** that integrate governing physics, multi-fidelity simulations, and limited real-world observations to make complex engineering systems observable, predictable, and actionable. My long-term goal is to **close the lifecycle loop** by advancing physics-informed digital twins into continuously evolving engineering intelligence that can **Design** reliable physical systems and their digital counterparts, **Decide** how to adapt and acquire information under uncertainty, and **Discover** missing physics and transferable knowledge from reality.

My research bridges fundamental methodological advances and real-world engineering applications, spanning **energy storage, advanced manufacturing, aerospace propulsion, and mechatronic systems.** Through these efforts, I aim to translate physics-informed intelligence into safer, more reliable, and more autonomous engineering systems. I welcome opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration and research engagement. Whether you're a fellow researcher, a student, or someone curious about my work, feel free to explore my research and reach out!

<strong style="color: #c62828;">I am currently on the 2026-2027 academic job market, seeking tenure-track Assistant Professor positions in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, and Systems Engineering.</strong>

## Research Interests

1. **Physics-informed AI & Scientific ML:** physics-informed machine learning, neural operators, multi-fidelity learning, generative models, scientific discovery
2. **Digital twin & Computational Engineering:** multi-physics modeling, finite-element simulation, surrogate modeling, model-data integration
3. **Engineering Design & Manufacturing:** design optimization, in-situ processing monitoring, quality control & reliability, autonomous manufacturing, uncertainty quantification
4. **Prognostics & Lifecycle Intelligence:** condition monitoring, failure diagnosis, health state estimation, remaining useful life prediction, lifecycle decision-making
